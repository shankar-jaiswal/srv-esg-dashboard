AI insight summary

AI is used as a prototype layer to summarize report data and create simple recommendations.

🤖
Insight 1

Climate progress is SRV’s strongest dashboard story.

The 94% reduction in Scope 1+2 intensity is the clearest positive result, so it should be visible at the top of the website.

🔎
Insight 2

Value-chain emissions are still important.

Construction has large supplier and material impacts, so the dashboard should keep Scope 3 and supplier responsibility visible.

⚠️
Insight 3

Safety should remain a priority KPI.

LTIF improved over the long term, but construction work is safety-sensitive, so the prototype keeps safety in the main KPI cards.
